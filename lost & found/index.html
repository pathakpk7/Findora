How CampusFind Works

A confidential three-step workflow designed for student privacy

Report in Seconds

Log misplaced items or turn in found belongings with precise campus location coordinates, category, and date.

Confidential Claiming

Claimants submit unique ownership proofs such as serial numbers or hidden markings, concealed from the public.

Verify & Handoff

The finder reviews submitted proof. Upon verification, contact details are safely unlocked to coordinate return.
